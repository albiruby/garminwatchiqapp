# NUSANTARA Kawung — Connect IQ Store listing (draft)

**NUSANTARA · Series 01 Batik · Living Heritage on Your Wrist**

## Short description
A Kawung batik watch face that blooms with your day. Steps turn the motif from quiet line-work to full gold.

## Description (EN)
Kawung is one of the classical motifs of Javanese batik: four oval petals around a single centre, repeated in a precise lattice. It is commonly associated with the Yogyakarta and Surakarta court traditions.

In NUSANTARA Kawung the motif is the progress indicator:
- **Dormant:** at 0 steps the full Kawung lattice is drawn as fine line-work. It is a complete design on its own.
- **Awakening:** petals gain gilded outlines, sweeping clockwise from 12 o'clock, ahead of your progress.
- **Blooming:** petals fill with gold as you approach your daily step goal.
- **Complete:** at 100% every unit blooms and a gold ring closes the dial.

Also: analog or digital time, four configurable slots (34 metrics: VO2 max, race predictors, Body Battery, weekly distance, weather and more), a battery jewel that turns red below 20%, four colorways (Keraton Gold, Bronze, Silver Indigo, Sogan Brown), three pattern densities, and a dedicated low-pixel Always-On mode that shifts every minute to protect AMOLED screens.

*Linking batik petals to step progress is our design interpretation, not a traditional meaning of the motif.*

## Deskripsi (ID)
Kawung adalah salah satu motif klasik batik Jawa: empat kelopak lonjong mengelilingi satu pusat, diulang dalam kisi yang teratur, dan sering dikaitkan dengan tradisi keraton Yogyakarta dan Surakarta.

Di NUSANTARA Kawung, motif itu sendiri adalah indikator progres. Pada 0 langkah, motif tampil utuh sebagai garis halus (Dormant). Kelopak lalu mendapat garis emas (Awakening), terisi emas (Blooming), dan pada 100% seluruh motif mekar dengan cincin emas (Complete).

*Pemetaan kelopak ke progres langkah adalah interpretasi desain, bukan makna tradisional motif.*
