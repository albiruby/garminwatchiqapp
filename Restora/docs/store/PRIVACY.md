# RESTORA — Privacy statement

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)

RESTORA runs entirely on your Garmin watch.

**What is stored.** For each session you start, the app stores on the watch: the session name, start time, active duration, which exercises were completed or skipped, and, if you turned on *HR & HRV*, heart rate values and calculated HRV values. Settings (haptics, countdown, motion options) are also stored on the watch. At most the 100 most recent sessions are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.

**What is not collected.** RESTORA has no account, no internet or phone connection, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. No data leaves the watch. The app does not read your location, contacts or other apps' data.

**Sensors.** Heart rate and beat-to-beat intervals are read from the watch's own sensor only while a session runs, and only when *HR & HRV* is turned on in Settings (it is off by default). Raw beat intervals are used to calculate HRV during the session and are not stored; only the resulting values are saved with the session.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling RESTORA from the watch (or from Garmin Connect) removes all of its stored data.

**Contact.** Questions about this statement: use the developer contact on the RESTORA Connect IQ Store page.
