## Description

RESTORA is a guided recovery companion for your Forerunner 165. Choose a short session, follow the timer and the animated figure, and let the watch guide you with gentle vibrations. Everything runs offline on the watch.

**Six modules, 33 guided sessions**

- **Breathe** — Box breathing, 4-7-8, extended exhale, resonance breathing and your own custom pattern, with a visual pacer.
- **Mind** — Mindful breathing, body scan, focus reset, mental unwind and gratitude reflection.
- **Circulate** — Ankle pumps, heel raises, seated marching and leg elevation for long days of sitting or standing.
- **Mobility** — Calf, hip, spine, shoulder and chest mobility, from the 90/90 hip rotation to the world's greatest stretch, with step-by-step figures.
- **Sleep** — Bedtime breathing, gentle night mobility, reclined butterfly, supine twist and supported rest before bed.
- **Recover** — Cooldowns and stretching after runs and workouts, from 5 to 20 minutes.

**Built for the watch**

- Anatomy X-Ray exercise figures: a dark scan-style body with the working muscle in red, for all 36 movements. The timer never depends on the animation.
- Haptic cues for each new exercise, side change and the end of a session.
- Button-first control: START to pause, DOWN to skip, UP to go back.
- Optional rest between exercises (10, 15 or 30 s).
- Optional heart rate and HRV: compare your heart rate and HRV (RMSSD) at the start and end of a session, using your watch's own sensor.
- History: your week at a glance, plus a detail page for each of your last 20 sessions.

Every number is labelled: **Garmin** for data measured by your watch, **RESTORA CALC** for values the app calculates. Missing data is shown as `--`, never estimated.

RESTORA is a wellness app, not a medical device. See the disclaimer below.

## Disclaimer (paste into the Store description)

RESTORA provides general wellness guidance for breathing, relaxation and gentle movement. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Heart rate and HRV values are for personal comparison only. Leg elevation is not suitable for everyone (for example with heart or kidney failure, glaucoma or uncontrolled blood pressure). Stop any exercise that causes pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, and ask a qualified health professional before starting if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from injury or surgery.
