## Description (EN)
```
Mega Mendung is the cloud motif of Cirebon batik: billowing clouds drawn in nested layers that step from a light rim to a deep core, with spiral curls inside. NUSANTARA Mega Mendung turns it into a living watch face – the sky itself is your daily progress.

LIVING PATTERN – RISING LAYERS
• Dormant – at 0 steps the clouds are drawn as fine line work. It is a complete design on its own.
• Awakening – every cloud layer is traced in light lines.
• Blooming – clouds fill with their full gradation, rising from the bottom of the dial as you approach your daily step goal.
• Complete – at 100% the whole sky is in colour and a ring closes the dial.
It resets at midnight and rises again with every step.

YOUR DATA, YOUR SLOTS
Four configurable slots with 34 metrics, set in Garmin Connect:
• Top: weekday + date, date, weather, temperature, sunrise, sunset, next calendar event
• Bottom: steps, calories, distance, active minutes, floors, battery, heart rate, respiration, Pulse Ox
• Left: running/cycling VO2 max, heart rate, Body Battery, stress, training status, recovery time, 5K/10K/half/marathon race predictors
• Right: weekly running/cycling distance, weekly active and intensity minutes, daily distance, calories and active minutes
Values come from your watch's own data. Metrics your watch does not support show "--".

DESIGN
• Analog or digital time, set in the Pridi typeface
• Four colorways: Cirebon Blue, Bronze, Cirebon Red, Sogan Brown
• Three pattern densities
• Battery jewel at 12 o'clock turns red below 20%
• Dedicated low-pixel Always-On mode that shifts every minute to protect the AMOLED screen

Note: linking the clouds to step progress is our design interpretation, not a traditional meaning of the motif.

Typeface: Pridi by Cadson Demak, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
```

## Deskripsi (ID)
```
Mega Mendung adalah motif awan khas batik Cirebon: awan bergulung yang digambar berlapis, bergradasi dari tepi terang ke inti gelap, dengan ukel spiral di dalamnya. NUSANTARA Mega Mendung menjadikannya watch face yang hidup – langit itu sendiri adalah progres harianmu.

LIVING PATTERN – RISING LAYERS
• Dormant – pada 0 langkah, awan tampil sebagai garis halus. Sudah menjadi desain yang utuh.
• Awakening – setiap lapisan awan tergambar dengan garis terang.
• Blooming – awan terisi gradasi penuh, naik dari bawah dial seiring langkah mendekati target harian.
• Complete – pada 100% seluruh langit berwarna dan cincin menutup dial.
Kembali ke awal setiap tengah malam dan naik lagi di setiap langkah.

DATAMU, SLOTMU
Empat slot yang bisa diatur dengan 34 metrik, lewat Garmin Connect:
• Atas: hari + tanggal, tanggal, cuaca, suhu, matahari terbit, matahari terbenam, agenda kalender berikutnya
• Bawah: langkah, kalori, jarak, menit aktif, lantai, baterai, detak jantung, laju napas, Pulse Ox
• Kiri: VO2 max lari/sepeda, detak jantung, Body Battery, stres, status latihan, waktu pemulihan, prediksi lomba 5K/10K/half/marathon
• Kanan: jarak lari/sepeda mingguan, menit aktif dan intensitas mingguan, jarak, kalori, dan menit aktif harian
Metrik yang tidak didukung jam akan tampil "--".

DESAIN
• Jam analog atau digital dengan huruf Pridi
• Empat warna: Biru Cirebon, Perunggu, Merah Cirebon, Coklat Sogan
• Tiga kerapatan motif
• Permata baterai di pukul 12 berubah merah di bawah 20%
• Mode Always-On hemat piksel yang bergeser tiap menit untuk melindungi layar AMOLED

Catatan: pemetaan awan ke progres langkah adalah interpretasi desain kami, bukan makna tradisional motif.

Huruf: Pridi oleh Cadson Demak, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
```
